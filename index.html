Property Overview

Top-Floor Penthouse Views
at 51 Innes Court

Top floor penthouse at The Shipyard’s coveted southwest corner offers stunning water and mountain views from the great room’s floor-to-ceiling windows, flooding the space with natural light.

The modern kitchen features stainless steel appliances, a gas stove, stone countertops, designer cabinetry, and a large center island — perfect for casual dining and entertaining. The home boasts two split bedrooms and two bathrooms, ensuring privacy. The en suite bedroom offers views of The Crane, a walk-in closet, and a contemporary bathroom with double vanities and a marble-walled shower. The hallway bathroom includes a tub and a stacked laundry closet. The home is equipped with both AC and heating.

Built in 2017 by Lennar, 51 Innes Court is a boutique waterfront building with 36 homes, featuring amenities like assigned garage parking, bike storage, a common patio with seating, BBQ, fire pits, landscaped walkways, and a playground.

Conveniently located near Dogpatch, Potrero Hill, and Mission Bay, with easy access to highways, and close to UCSF Medical Center and sports stadiums. This is an exceptional opportunity to enjoy modern living with a prime location.
